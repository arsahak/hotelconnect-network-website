# HotelConnect Network

Website for HotelConnect Network, a private B2B platform where boutique hotels and verified suppliers find each other. It covers compliance, tenders, jobs and group purchasing.

Hotels keep track of their contracts, permits and certificates with automatic reminders, send tenders to suppliers in their category and region, and post jobs that candidates can apply to in one click via WhatsApp or email. Suppliers receive targeted requests instead of cold calling, get their own profile in the supplier directory, and can book corporate vouchers at member hotels.

The public site includes a hotel guide where guests book directly with the hotel, an open jobs page, and a supplier directory. Members also get a private forum and join group purchasing deals, starting with energy. The platform is currently in its test phase, with a waitlist for new hotels and suppliers.
